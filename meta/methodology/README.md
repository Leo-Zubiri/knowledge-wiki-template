# meta/methodology/ — why this repository is shaped the way it is

Three short documents define the method. Read them in order:

1. [`methodologies.md`](methodologies.md) — the four methodologies we draw on (CODE, PARA, Zettelkasten, LLM-Wiki),
   what each one *owns*, and what we deliberately adapt or reject.
2. [`lifecycle.md`](lifecycle.md) — how information moves from capture to verified knowledge: the content classes,
   the review states, and the governance rule that keeps observations separate from verified knowledge.
3. [`metadata.md`](metadata.md) — the portable YAML frontmatter conventions (the three independent dimensions),
   every field's purpose, and worked examples.

These are **recommendations and conventions**, grounded where possible in primary sources (cited inline). They are
the "why"; the [`../workflows/`](../workflows/) are the "how".
